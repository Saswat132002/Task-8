# 📊 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

Welcome to the **Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard** project! This repository showcases a business intelligence dashboard developed to analyze and visualize key sales metrics from a Superstore dataset.

---

## 🔍 Overview

This interactive dashboard provides insights into:

- ✅ **Total Sales**, **Profit Margin**, and **Customer Retention Rate**
- 🌍 **Sales Breakdown by Region and Country**
- 🛍️ **Top-Selling Products and Product Categories**
- 📈 **Monthly Sales Trends**
- 🚚 **Shipping Mode Analysis**
- 🧾 **Order Volume Summary**

All visuals are designed to help stakeholders quickly understand performance, spot trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Central Region** leads in total sales, crossing the $1M mark.
- **Technology** is the highest-selling category, followed by Office Supplies.
- The **Priority** shipping mode is used in over 60% of orders.
- Consistent **growth trend in sales** from 2011 to 2014 is evident.
- **France**, **Germany**, and the **UK** are top-performing countries.
